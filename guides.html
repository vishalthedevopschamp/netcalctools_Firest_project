Suggested reading order

If you are new to subnetting, read the subnet mask cheat sheet first so the mask and prefix notation make sense, then the /24 guide, which is the network size you will meet most often. Move on to /27 to see how a /24 is divided, and /16 to see how many /24s fit into a larger block. Finish with private IP ranges, which explains why some addresses never appear on the public internet and how to avoid overlaps when you plan your own.
